## Summary

KAIRAS does not sell generic fixed software plans. Pricing depends on the process, volume, channels, integrations and level of support required by each SME.

Most projects start with a diagnostic or scoped map, then move into implementation and optional maintenance.

## General Pricing Logic

KAIRAS prices automation and applied AI work by scope. The main factors are:

- Number of processes to automate.
- Number of tools to connect.
- Channels involved, such as WhatsApp, email, CRM, calendar or documents.
- Data quality and current process clarity.
- Need for human review, escalation and monitoring.
- Monthly volume of leads, messages, appointments or documents.
- Support and maintenance after launch.

## What Is Included In A Typical Project

- Process diagnosis and prioritization.
- Definition of the first automation flow.
- Setup of integrations and rules.
- Copy and response logic for customer-facing messages when needed.
- CRM, task or follow-up structure when needed.
- Testing before launch.
- Human escalation rules.
- Basic measurement of impact: time saved, pending work, response continuity or lost opportunities.

## What KAIRAS Does Not Promise

- No guaranteed ranking, sales or revenue figures.
- No generic AI system without human control.
- No replacement of clinical, legal, financial or strategic human judgement.
- No hidden fixed price for complex integrations.
