## Audience

KAIRAS is built for business owners and operators who need clearer systems, fewer manual tasks and better follow-up. The content is written for non-technical decision makers, not for developers looking for generic tutorials.

## Differentiation

KAIRAS prioritizes process clarity before tools. Its approach is to decide what should be automated, what should remain human and how to connect existing tools without creating more manual work.
